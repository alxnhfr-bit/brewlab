Support

15GRMS is a brewing companion: guided recipes, a wall clock timer that survives a locked screen, and a journal that fills itself in.

Need a hand, found a bug, or want a recipe added? Open an issue at github.com/alxnhfr-bit/brewlab/issues and you will get a reply. No account details are needed to report a problem.

Common questions

Does the timer keep running if I lock my phone?

Yes. Each step runs against the clock rather than a counter, and notifications for every remaining step are scheduled in advance, so locking your phone or switching apps will not lose your place. Allow notifications when asked and each step will alert you.

Can I change the dose or ratio for a recipe?

Open a recipe and tap Dial in. Adjust the dose, ratio or temperature and every pour target recalculates as you go. Your change is remembered for next time.

How do I stop a brew I have already started?

Tap the cross at the top left of the brewing screen, then choose End brew. Choosing Keep brewing instead tucks the timer into a bar above the tabs so it carries on while you use the rest of the app.

How do I delete a brew from my journal?

Swipe the row to the left and tap Delete. You can also open the brew and use Delete at the bottom of its detail screen.

What are the taste chips for?

After a brew you can tag how it tasted. If something was off, the app suggests one conservative adjustment, and if you save it, the tweak is applied the next time you brew that recipe.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Library, then the settings icon, then Export journal exports everything as JSON.

Does it work offline?

Entirely. Recipes and fonts are bundled in the app and it makes no network requests at all.

Privacy

15GRMS collects nothing. Everything you record stays on your device. See the privacy policy for details.
