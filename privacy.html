Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

15GRMS does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third party services. The app does not send anything anywhere, because it has no server to send it to.

What the app stores

Everything you create in 15GRMS stays on your device:

  • Your brew journal, including ratings, taste notes and any text you write
  • Your favourite recipes
  • Remembered doses and saved brewing adjustments
  • Your appearance settings, and whether haptics and sound are on

This information is written to your device's local storage. It is never transmitted, never backed up to us, and never seen by anyone but you. We could not access it even if asked, because it never leaves your phone.

Network access

The app makes no network requests. Recipes and fonts are bundled inside the app itself, so it works fully offline, including in a kitchen with no signal.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, 15GRMS schedules them locally on your device so that each brewing step can alert you while your screen is off. These are created and delivered entirely by your device's operating system. No notification content is sent over the internet, and we receive no record that a notification was scheduled or shown.

Permissions the app may request

  • Notifications, so brewing steps can alert you in the background. Optional, and the app works without them.
  • Keeping the screen awake during an active brew, so the timer stays visible. This is switched on only while you are brewing.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or health data.

Children

15GRMS is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual brews by swiping a row in the Journal, or from a brew's detail screen. Deleting the app from your device removes everything it has stored, permanently and immediately. There is no copy anywhere else.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds optional accounts or syncing, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here rather than applied quietly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised at github.com/alxnhfr-bit/brewlab/issues.
