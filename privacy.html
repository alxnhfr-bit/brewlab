Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 September 2026

15GRMS does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The app does not send anything anywhere, because it has no server to send it to. The only third party you can reach from it is the feedback form, and only if you choose to open it.

What the app stores

Everything you create in 15GRMS stays on your device:

  • Your brew journal, including ratings, taste notes and any text you write
  • Your favourite recipes
  • Remembered doses and saved brewing adjustments
  • Your appearance settings, and whether haptics and sound are on

This information is written to your device's local storage. The app never transmits it, never backs it up to us, and we could not access it even if asked, because it has nowhere to send it.

The one way anything leaves your phone is if you choose to send it. Settings contains an Export journal option, which writes your journal as a JSON file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there, Files, Mail, AirDrop or anywhere else, is entirely your choice, and a copy you export is outside this policy because it is no longer in the app's hands.

Network access

The app makes no network requests. Recipes and fonts are bundled inside the app itself, so it works fully offline, including in a kitchen with no signal.

The one exception is a link you tap yourself. Settings contains links to this policy, to the support page and to the feedback form. Tapping one leaves the app and opens the page in Safari, and from that point the site you have opened applies its own privacy policy, not this one. The app is not involved in that request and learns nothing about it.

The feedback form is worth naming specifically, because it is the only third party service involved anywhere. It is hosted by Tally. If you open and submit it, Tally receives whatever you type and, like any web server, your IP address. Everything on the form is optional except the message itself, so you can report a problem without telling us who you are. Leave an email address only if you would like a reply.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, 15GRMS schedules them locally on your device so that each brewing step can alert you while your screen is off. These are created and delivered entirely by your device's operating system. No notification content is sent over the internet, and we receive no record that a notification was scheduled or shown.

Permissions the app may request

  • Notifications, so brewing steps can alert you in the background. Asked for only when a brew is long enough to need it, which in practice means cold brew steeping for ten to twelve hours. Optional, and the app works without them.
  • Keeping the screen awake through a step you are watching, so the timer stays visible while you pour. It is released for long steeps, so an overnight cold brew does not hold your display on all night.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or health data.

Children

15GRMS is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual brews by swiping a row in the Journal, or from a brew's detail screen. Deleting the app from your device removes everything it has stored, permanently and immediately. We hold no copy, because we never received one. Any file you exported yourself is yours to delete wherever you put it.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds optional accounts or syncing, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here rather than applied quietly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the feedback form, which needs no account. If you prefer a public tracker, the repository is at github.com/alxnhfr-bit/brewlab/issues.
